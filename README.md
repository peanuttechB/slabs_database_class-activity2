1. Transaction Processing System (TPS)

A Trasaction Processing System records and processes daily business transactions at SuperMart SA. At the checkout counters, it records customer purchases, payments, receipts, returns and refunds.
At the inventory warehouse, it records stock received, products dispatched and changes in stock quantities. These transactions must be recorded accurately and reliably to ensure that sales and inventory 
information remains up to date.

2. Management Information System (MIS) and Decision Support Systems (DSS)

An MIS helps store managers track weekly sales performance by providing reports on total sales, best-selling products, department performance and sales targets. This helps managers monitor the overall 
performance of the store and plan business activities.

A DSS helps managers make decisions when problems occur. For example, if sales suddenly decrease, the DSS can analyse sales trends and customer demand to help managers decide whether introducing promotions or
discounts would increase sales.

3. Executive Support Systems (ESS)

An ESS helps top-level executives at SuperMart SA make long-term strategic decisions. The CEO can use an ESS to view high-level reports on the company's overall performance, competitor information and long term sales forecasts. It can also provide static reports and on-demand graphs to identify business trends and opportunities. This information helps the CEO plan for future growth, monitor competitors and make 
informed business decisions.

4. Knowledge Work Systems (KWS) and Office Automation Systems (OAS)

A Knowledge Work System (KWS) helps employees create and apply new knowledge in business activities. For example, designers at SuperMart SA can use Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software to create store layouts 
and product displays.

An Office Automation System (OAS) helps employees complete daily administrative tasks. For example, SuperMart SA can use Microsoft Office tools such as Word and Excel to create documents, manage information 
and prepare reports.

5. IT Landscape and Legacy Systems 

5.1 Why connecting legacy systems to cloud applications increase complexity.
Connecting old legacy systems to modern cloud applications increase complexity because they may use different technologies, software versions and data formats. This can make communication and data sharing
difficult. The company may also need additional integration tools, maintenance and technical support to ensure that all systems work together.

5.2 Two ways SuperMart SA can save costs using an IT Landscape Diagram

- End-of-Life asset tracking: The company can identify outdated hardware and software that need replacement. This helps management plan upgrades and reduce maintenance costs.
- Standardisation: The company can identify systems performing similar functions and use standardised software and hardware. Thus reduces duplicatedsystems, licensing costs and maintenance expenses.
